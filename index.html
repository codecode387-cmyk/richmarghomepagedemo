How the Solar EPC Process Works

A clear path from enquiry to installation and support.

1

Initial Consultation

You explain your location, energy needs, and the appliances or equipment you want to power.

2

Energy Requirements Assessment

Electricity consumption, operating hours, backup requirements, and installation conditions are reviewed.

3

System Design & Quotation

A proposed system configuration and quotation are prepared based on the assessment and available equipment.

4

Installation & Commissioning

Where included in the agreed contract, the system is installed, checked, and commissioned.

5

Handover & Support

Operating guidance and any after-installation support confirmed by the company.
